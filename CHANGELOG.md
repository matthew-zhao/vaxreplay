## 0.1.0-alpha.1 — 2026-07-24

Initial technical preview of infrastructure for temporally controlled biomedical evaluation:

- no admitted real-data benchmark or official leaderboard;
- Apache-2.0 for software and documentation, CC BY 4.0 for project-authored fictional fixtures,
  DCO 1.1, and GitHub private vulnerability reporting;
- a private `vaxreplay-dev` development repository and fresh public `vaxreplay` repository;
- task, evidence, forecast, citation, and scoring contracts;
- deterministic evaluation and aggregation;
- historical-source adapter interfaces;
- contamination-control and temporal-admission protocols;
- development-only sealed-runner and provider-gateway components;
- a sanitized seven-command research CLI surface;
- unit tests and deterministic smoke tests; and
- wholly fictional antigen and candidate-ranking conformance fixtures.

This release contains no real IEDB, AACT/ClinicalTrials.gov, ImmPort, VaxSeer, or FluSelect
evaluation data; no internal real-task cohort or private gold; and no scientific model leaderboard.
